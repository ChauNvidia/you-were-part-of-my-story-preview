For Larry Griffith

You wrote to us.
We are writing back.

On August 14th, Larry, you wrote a letter to 20,000 scholars. 37 scholars wrote back, and this is where those letters live.

You have been a longtime champion of the Gates Millennium Scholars program, and Scholars Equity Network wanted you to see what grew from it: classrooms, clinics, courtrooms, kitchens, boardrooms, and a community that keeps reaching back for the next person.

You would be the first to say you were one of many. There was a counselor who handed someone an application. A teacher who said you should try. A family member who made room. A stranger who quietly opened a door. This gift is for them too.

With gratitude,
Scholars Equity Network
