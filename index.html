The Room Where It Happened

Larry saw what happened when we came together.

During his closing remarks at the 2026 Pay It Forward Summit, Larry Griffith watched scholars spend the day learning from one another and imagining what they could build together. Inspired by what he saw, he wrote a letter to more than 20,000 Gates Millennium Scholars. Many wrote back.

Larry has long been a champion of the Gates Millennium Scholars program, a partnership among four organizations: UNCF, the Hispanic Scholarship Fund, the Asian & Pacific Islander American Scholarship Fund, and Native Forward Scholars Fund.

In their letters, scholars thanked Larry and shared what the scholarship made possible—not just in their own lives, but in the classrooms, clinics, courtrooms, kitchens, boardrooms, and communities where they now make a difference.

Their stories remind us how far one investment can reach, and how that impact continues as scholars open doors for others.

We want to share these stories more broadly because they're not just a tribute to Larry. They're a reminder of how much it matters when someone believes in another person, creates an opportunity, or opens a door, often without ever knowing how far that act will reach.

Thank you, Larry, and thank you to everyone who has poured so much into us.

None of us got here alone

Illustration. Each small light stands for someone who helped. We have not mapped real relationships.

We began in a room, together. Larry saw what happened, and we wrote back.

As we reflected on the opportunities his leadership helped make possible, we were reminded of something bigger.

None of us got here alone.

Behind every Gates Millennium Scholar is a constellation of people who believed in us.

Some gave their time: a counselor who handed us the application.
Some gave their talent: a teacher who wrote a recommendation.
Some gave what they could least afford: a family member who made sacrifices.

And someone saw possibility in us before we could see it ourselves.

Many of those people may never know how far their kindness traveled.

What if we told them?

Keep the ripples going

Now it's our turn
to open the doors.

The next generation is waiting for someone to believe in them.

None of us arrived here alone. The opportunities that changed our lives existed because someone chose to invest in people they might never meet, whose futures they couldn't yet imagine.

Today, we are those people.

Somewhere, there's a student with the same potential we had, waiting for someone to see it. A young person who needs encouragement, a recommendation, a connection, or an opportunity they don't yet know exists.

The doors that opened for us won't automatically remain open for them.

We have to keep opening them.

That's why we're building Scholars Equity Network, a community of more than 20,000 Gates Millennium Scholars alumni from all four partner communities. The scholarship invested in each of us as individuals. We exist so scholars can invest in one another, through mentorship, leadership, and the doors we open for each other. Through the Pay It Forward Accelerator and the Pay It Forward Summit we turn those acts into lasting community impact.

What can you pay forward? Your time, your talent, your treasure. Start by thanking someone who helped you.
